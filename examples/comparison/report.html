Scope differences that matter

Native trial clicks can scroll a clipped or off-screen target into view. Our rule inspects its current center after declared setup. Fully transparent and inert ancestors also have different native preconditions. All 4 control disagreements are retained below.

  • Ancestor clips the action center: our rule failed; native checks passed.
  • Inert application region: our rule inconclusive; native checks failed.
  • Fully transparent ancestor: our rule inconclusive; native checks passed.
  • Unscrolled off-screen action: our rule inconclusive; native checks passed.

Renderlint audits broader layout conditions. Here it reports cart-background text covered by the open popup even when checkout is usable. Those warnings are not scored as action failures or automatically dismissed as noise. Its chosen profile produced findings in 8 states, with 0 unresolved analyzer runs.

Method and raw evidence

Each method uses a fresh context with identical starting input, viewport, locale and declared setup. Native trial clicks have a 600ms timeout. Renderlint 0.2.0 runs its supported in-place API with one viewport/light scheme and a documented layout subset; stress, sweeps and data mutation are disabled. It may scroll or change focus while auditing. A pass-through wrapper records analyzer errors/meta.

Preparation/measurement timings and before/after snapshots are in the raw data. Analyzer injection precedes its measurement phase, and workloads differ. Do not use these timings as a speed ranking. No browser page errors: verified.
